This image illustrates an Arduino Uno connected to a series of LEDs through resistors on a breadboard. Each LED is linked to a separate digital output pin of the Arduino, allowing the LEDs to be controlled individually through programming.

In this setup, the LEDs switch ON and OFF in a directional sequence starting from the right side and moving toward the left side. After the last LED on the left lights up, the sequence reverses direction, causing the LEDs to turn ON and OFF from left to right again. This repeated back-and-forth movement creates a “running light” or “ping-pong” lighting effect.

The circuit demonstrates important Arduino concepts such as:

Digital output control.
Sequential LED programming.
Timing using delay functions.
Looping and directional control in code.
